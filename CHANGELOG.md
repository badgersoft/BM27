# Changelog

## v0.9.1 (2026-10-18)
- The commit-message hook no longer stamps merge commits.

## v0.9.0 (2026-10-18): Longer careers and a lot more variety
- The Final Tour is optional and only available after ten years together; bands can carry on for decades.
- Small venues (village hall, school hall, pub, student union) have different names in each city and each game.
- Three times as many song and album titles, twice the band-name words and more name patterns, more national and local rival bands, twice as many first names for the public.
- More newspaper headlines and standfirsts, three times the gig reviews and critic quotes, twice the crowd reactions, three times the documentary and concert film titles.
- The song-feel slider handle is a pair of beamed sixteenth notes.
- Titles you type for your own songs and albums are capitalised in the same style as the game's own titles.
- The month and week under NEXT WEEK is a touch bigger and brighter.

## v0.8.0 (2026-10-11): Version numbers
- Every commit gets a version number (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH); see "Versions" in the README.
- The version is shown on the main menu, and stamped into every export preset.

## v0.7.2 (2026-10-11)
- Crowd pints are dimmer and see-through, so they read as glasses.

## v0.7.1 (2026-10-11)
- Stage art: the band stands on the stage, the drummer sits clear of the singer and the band name, and pints replace the festival flags.

## v0.7.0 (2026-10-11): Venue stage art
- Each venue size has its own gig-night look: village hall, pub/club, theatre, arena, stadium (and a pyramid stage at the festival).
- The gig screen separates "new listeners" from "became fans", and good gigs properly win over walk-ins.

## v0.6.1 (2026-10-11)
- A gig-night capture mode for the screenshot tool (`--gigshots`).

## v0.6.0 (2026-10-11): City scenes
- Every city leads with a different genre, and each game shakes the scenes up a little.

## v0.5.0 (2026-10-11): Seven genres, song feel and demo songs
- Genres cut to seven; musicians have a second favourite genre.
- Song feel slider (Poppy to Dial Up To 11) when naming a release.
- Whole demo songs replace the song bank.
- National Top 20, Local Top 10, records for weeks at No.1, no duplicate titles, more bands and titles.
- Tougher critic with advice; event cooldowns and new events.

## v0.4.0 (2026-10-10): Member fame
- Every band member has their own fame, and famous members get up to things (good and bad).

## v0.3.0 (2026-10-10): Playtest feedback
- "Who Are You?" manager screen, Odd Jobs and favour gigs, randomised bar takings, fewer mishaps, fresher newspapers, scroll hints and more.

## v0.2.1 (2026-10-10)
- The public is counted on a tiered representation curve (about 10 million UK music lovers).

## v0.2.0 (2026-10-10): The public base
- 29,000 named people across 9 UK regions, a home city, and fans who spread local, regional, then national.

## v0.1.1 (2026-10-10)
- Gig art and name polish, modal fixes, early-game test shots.

## v0.1.0 (2026-10-10): First playable
- Band Manager 27 in Godot 4.7 for PC and mobile.
