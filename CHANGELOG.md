# Changelog

## v0.11.0 (2026-10-18): Albums, archive tabs and fewer star cameos
- Albums are harder to chart (a first album might just scrape the Top 20, or miss), and sell about a fifth as many copies as a single at five times the price.
- A Top 10 single revives your latest album, sending it back up the album chart.
- The release archive has Singles and Albums tabs, and marks releases that didn't chart.
- Naming a release: suggestions are selectable (the first is picked already) and RELEASE IT always works; the dialog is more compact so every suggestion fits.
- Stars ask to join far less often; documentary and concert film offers only come after five years together, and rarely.
- Top fans show a surname initial ("Dave C").

## v0.10.1 (2026-10-18)
- Talent page: the tags under each musician wrap, so rows fit on narrow screens (XL interface size).

## v0.10.0 (2026-10-18): Harder charts and band treats
- Treat the whole band: a Night on the Town or a Spa Weekend (Band page).
- Treat messages stay on screen longer; longer messages stay up longer generally.
- Hiring opens the talent page on the empty seat instead of always the singer.
- Fans page: the left-hand boxes no longer run off the screen as numbers grow; once you're national, other regions show as one combined line each.
- Gig offers: the cards share the width equally and never run off the screen.
- Charts are ranked on sales: a new single only charts once its first week's sales are in, and each chart row shows that week's sales.
- National chart is now a Top 30, with stronger rival records and more sales needed to get in; famous bands no longer get an automatic No.1.

## v0.9.1 (2026-10-18)
- The commit-message hook no longer stamps merge commits.

## v0.9.0 (2026-10-18): Longer careers and a lot more variety
- The Final Tour is optional and only available after ten years together; bands can carry on for decades.
- Small venues (village hall, school hall, pub, student union) have different names in each city and each game.
- Three times as many song and album titles, twice the band-name words and more name patterns, more national and local rival bands, twice as many first names for the public.
- More newspaper headlines and standfirsts, three times the gig reviews and critic quotes, twice the crowd reactions, three times the documentary and concert film titles.
- The song-feel slider handle is a pair of beamed sixteenth notes.
- Titles you type for your own songs and albums are capitalised in the same style as the game's own titles.
- The month and week under NEXT WEEK is a touch bigger and brighter.
